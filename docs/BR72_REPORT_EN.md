## Scope and limitations

The research covered 17,564 resolved five-minute Bitcoin Up/Down markets and 1,050 research variants, including entry-price limits, fees, base slippage, doubled slippage and two consecutive monthly blocks.

This report does not prove future profitability, exact live fills, unlimited liquidity, results at larger stakes or uninterrupted operation of Polymarket, the VPS or the network. It is a historical calculation on observed market data, not a brokerage statement.
